FAQ

Common Questions

I have an injury or pain — can I still train?
Almost certainly yes. The research is clear: progressive loading is one of the most effective approaches for most movement and performance limitations. We'll identify what's actually going on and build around it — not away from it.
How much time do I need to commit per week?
Programs are designed around your availability. Most clients train 3–4 days per week, 45–60 minutes per session. The weekly check-in takes about 5 minutes. Online meetings are scheduled based on your needs and progress.
Do I need a gym? What equipment is required?
A commercial gym gives us the most tools to work with, but programs can be adapted to home gyms with barbells and dumbbells. We discuss this during the initial call so I can build around what you have.
What makes this different from a personal trainer or other online coaches?
Most coaches guess. I use updated research and a science-based approach to inform every decision — from exercise selection to load management to recovery. I also understand rehabilitation, which means I can address movement and performance issues more effectively than a standard personal trainer.
What happens after I apply?
Once you submit your application, you'll be directed to book a free intro call. We'll talk through your goals, your situation, and make sure we're aligned before getting started. No pressure, no hard sell.
How do the packages work?
Coaching is offered in structured packages — real change takes time, and the programs are built around that. We'll discuss the right option for you on the call.
